# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Prioritize the health of the community and project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publication of private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communications that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact channel documented in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed confidentially and fairly.
